## How to read these tables

- **Period type** - `instant` is a balance at a single date (balance-sheet items work this way); `duration` is a flow measured across a range (income-statement and cash-flow items).
- **Accession** - the filing that reported this value. The same period often appears in several filings: the original, then again as a comparative in later ones.
- **Frame / fy / fp** - EDGAR's own fiscal labelling of the *filing* the fact came from, not of the fact's period. They disagree more often than you would like.
- Units are as reported. Nothing here has been rescaled.

Facts are limited to periods ending on or after `2021-01-01`, which covers every column of every filing fetched for Phase 1.

Accession numbers belonging to the four filings you are reading by hand are marked **(fetched)**.

Only concepts describing money Apple **owes** are tabulated below. Concepts about debt securities Apple **holds** as investments are listed at the end instead - they match a search for the word 'debt' and mean the opposite thing, which is the first trap in this data.
